# Anchor Home Services — Product Context

**Register:** brand
**Product Purpose:** A portfolio-grade demonstration of a dependable, dispatch-smart HVAC and plumbing service for Greater Providence homeowners. The site makes operational truth visible before booking: clear service boundaries, technician-aware scheduling, and an explicit distinction between emergency and planned work.

**Users:**
- Homeowners in Greater Providence (02903–02916 corridor) needing heating, cooling, or plumbing help who want a calm, specific answer rather than a callback queue
- Evaluate/compare visitors assessing Anchor’s reliability, coverage, and maintenance plans before committing
- Mobile-first users (on a couch, in a basement looking at a leak) who need to know in under 30 seconds whether Anchor covers their address and how fast help arrives

**Brand Pillars:**
- Clarity is hospitality — every decision gets an immediate, plainly written answer
- Operational details are the brand — status labels, time windows, and technician availability are proof of competence
- Quiet, tactile precision — fine grain, sturdy outlines, measured spacing, modest motion

**Design Movement:** Field Manual Modernism — mid-century operational manual pragmatism meets contemporary product restraint. A dispatch desk, not a contractor cliché.

**Differentiators:**
- Coverage check before lead capture (honest 15-mile corridor)
- Capacity-tied scheduling (each slot belongs to a real technician)
- Emergency vs standard lanes with different response promises and pricing

**Tone:** Composed, capable, straight-talking. Direct operational language: “Tell us what’s happening. We’ll route the right response.” No hype, no vague “contact us.”

**Anti-References:**
- No warm-cream contractor template with stock hero and centered blocks
- No generic SaaS dash or neo-brutalist gimmick
- No glassmorphism hero, gradient text, or side-stripe accent as decoration
- No endless identical icon+heading+text card grids

**Strategic Principles:**
- Asymmetry earns attention — dispatch rail offset, image fields crossing the content plane
- Anchor Line (cobalt line with circular terminus) connects navigation, sections, and booking progress
- Job Tickets (square-cornered, softly shadowed cards with coded header strips) for services, slots, and plans
- Field Marks (compact uppercase labels, short rule lines, modest grid texture)

**Primary Entry Points:** / (home hero with live dispatch), /services (catalog + coverage check), /book (two-lane booking flow), /plans (maintenance), /about (standards + contact)
